How to read these trends

The default selection is the device with the newest usable drive date under the branch and date policy filters. In individual mode, each device has its own colored line on the same City, Road, and Highway charts. Colors match the device legend and weekly distance bars. Hover a week to compare devices, or focus a point for details. Choose “Combined devices” to pool their totals. Clear the device selection to return to the newest device.

Each point combines the preceding {{ trends.window_weeks * 7 }} calendar days, ending on its labeled date. Historical points end on Sundays; the final point includes today so far. Adjacent windows overlap. The bars show distance driven in each individual week, so you can distinguish an empty week from an empty rolling window.

Distance engagement = total engaged kilometres ÷ total driven kilometres. Time engagement = total engaged time ÷ total time, including stopped time. Rates = total events ÷ total driven kilometres × 100. Only drives with both required fields contribute. Percentages are calculated from totals, so longer drives carry their appropriate weight.

Orange rings mark windows containing dates estimated from uploads. Offline drives may arrive together and appear in their upload week. Choose “Recording dates only” to remove them. Plausible recording dates pass clock and software-date checks; this cannot prove the device clock is correct. Undated drives remain outside the timeline.

Hollow points indicate less than {{ trends.low_distance_km }} km in a window; this is a volume cue, not a statistical confidence interval. Gaps mean no usable data. Changes compare the current window with the equally long, non-overlapping window immediately before it. Branch filters apply across the complete timeline; route, weather, and driving choices can also affect engagement.
