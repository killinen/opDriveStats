[ Stat Guide ]

Total Drives

Count of uploaded drives that made it into this build. Each entry is one trip with valid logs.

Total Distance

All kilometers recorded, engaged or not. Acts as the odometer baseline for everything else.

Total Engaged Distance

Kilometers where assisted driving was active. Comparing this to total distance yields ODO engagement.

Total Active Hours

Minutes spent with the system available or engaged, converted to hours for easier scanning.

ODO Engagement

Engaged kilometers divided by overall kilometers. Higher means the system is trusted for more of the route.

Time Engagement

Engaged time divided by total active time. Complements the distance view when traffic slows things down.

Total Disengagements

Every time the driver took over or the system exited by itself. Watch the per-100 km metric for normalization.

Disengagements / 100km

Reliability indicator. Keeps comparisons fair even if the fleet size or distance changes.

Total Steer Interventions

Counts steering nudges or overrides outside of full disengagements. Early signal for comfort issues.

Steer Interventions / 100km

Same normalization as disengagements so spikes stand out regardless of trip length.

Speed Buckets

Breaks the totals into velocity bands. Engagement percentages inside each bucket show what happens in traffic vs highway.
