Drive evidence

Initial temperature is the first valid reported reading within 30 seconds of a known route start. Δ values compare with the temperature at 5, 15 or 30 minutes; unavailable observations remain blank. This does not measure parked cabin temperature or isolate device self-heating.

Coverage and interpretation

TRQI temperature expires after 1.5 seconds, speed and control context after 2 seconds, outside temperature after 60 seconds, and HVAC state after 5 seconds. Invalid readings and missing segments remain gaps. A 30-second mean requires 30 consecutive valid seconds. Exposure durations use the one-second grid and are approximate.

Outside temperature × phase is a weather comparison. MCU minus outside temperature is not a self-heating estimate. Blower/A/C requests do not establish actual cooling. Firmware “Unknown” means application identity was absent from the recorded logs. Installation and hardware revisions are not inferred.
